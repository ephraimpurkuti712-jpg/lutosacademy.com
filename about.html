About Lotus Academy

Fostering academic excellence, moral leadership, and comprehensive English-medium education in Simara, Bara since 2057 B.S.

Over 25 Years of Legacy

Shaping the Brightest Minds in Madhesh Province

Established in 2057 B.S., Lotus Academy was founded with a singular conviction: to deliver world-class English-medium education to students in Simara and surrounding regions of Bara, Parsa, and Rautahat.

Over the past quarter-century, our institution has blossomed from a humble school into a distinguished educational hub offering higher secondary (+2) Management and Science streams affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB).

Our Mission

Empower every student with knowledge, technical proficiency, ethical grounding, and creative confidence to thrive globally.

Our Vision

To stand as Nepal's most respected community-rooted academic institution that produces visionary entrepreneurs, scientists, and civil leaders.
