World-Class Infrastructure

Facilities Built for Holistic Learning

We provide our students in Simara with the modern facilities, technology, and spaces needed to excel intellectually and physically.

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped separate Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories enabling students to conduct practical experiments safely under expert guidance.

High-Speed ICT Computer Lab

Modern desktop workstations connected to high-speed internet, empowering students with coding, software applications, and digital literacy.

Resourceful Central Library

Extensive catalog of reference books, textbooks, literature, encyclopedias, and quiet study areas fostering a lifelong love of reading.

Safe Transportation Fleet

Dedicated school buses operating across Simara, Jitpur, Pathlaiya, and surrounding areas ensuring punctual and secure commute for students.

Sports & Athletics Ground

Spacious grounds for football, cricket, badminton, table tennis, and indoor games, with coaching for district and national championships.

Smart Digital Classrooms

Interactive multimedia projection systems that make complex concepts visual, engaging, and memorable for young minds.
